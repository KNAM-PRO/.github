## What we do

KNAM is a B2B lead-generation company. Our platform uses big data, predictive analytics and machine learning to find people who are ready to buy, and turns them into qualified leads for your sales team.

## Products and services

| | |
|---|---|
| **KNAM license** | Run the platform in-house and generate leads without intermediaries |
| **Turnkey lead generation** | Lead collection and transfer, including call-center processing |
| **Raw segmented leads** | Lead collection without processing, delivered as segmented contacts |
| **Live database** | A continuously updated base of potential customers |
| **Website mirror** | Recover lost and unfinished inquiries from your site |
| **Industry solutions** | Automotive, dental, real estate, travel, legal and more |
